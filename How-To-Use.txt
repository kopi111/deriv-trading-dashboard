DERIV TRADING DASHBOARD - HOW TO USE
=====================================

Website: https://kopi111.github.io/deriv-trading-dashboard/

This app gives you LIVE trading signals for Volatility Indices (V10, V25, V50, V75, V100).


3 PAGES TO USE:
===============

1. LIVE DASHBOARD (Main Page)
   - Shows live candlestick chart
   - Pick your index from dropdown (V10, V25, V50, V75, V100)
   - Pick timeframe (1M, 5M, 15M, 1H, 4H, 1D)
   - RSI indicator at bottom shows when to buy/sell

2. PRICE ACTION (Click "Price Action" link)
   - Shows cards for each index
   - Green = BUY signal
   - Red = SELL signal
   - Yellow = WAIT (no signal)
   - Click any card to see the chart with entry points

3. RSI STRATEGY (Click "RSI Strategy" link)
   - Uses backtested settings that actually work
   - Shows BUY when RSI drops too low
   - Shows SELL when RSI goes too high
   - Each index has different settings (already optimized)


HOW TO TRADE:
=============

Step 1: Open RSI Strategy page
Step 2: Look for BUY or SELL signals (not WAIT)
Step 3: Click the card to see Entry, Stop Loss, Take Profit
Step 4: Place your trade on Deriv with those levels

V50 - Best win rate (53.7%)

RSI SETTINGS (Already Built In):
================================

V10:  Buy when RSI < 20, Sell when RSI > 80

V50:  Buy when RSI < 35, Sell when RSI > 65  <-- BEST

TIPS:
=====

- Wait for ACTIVE signals (BUY or SELL), don't force trades
- Always use the Stop Loss shown
- 15-minute timeframe works best
- Refresh the page to get latest signals
- Works on phone too!
